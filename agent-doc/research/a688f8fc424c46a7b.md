## TOPIC 1 — Interactive Imitation Learning / DAgger family

### 1.1 Core methods

**DAgger** — *A Reduction of Imitation Learning and Structured Prediction to No-Regret Online Learning*, Ross, Gordon, Bagnell, AISTATS 2011.
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.0686 · PMLR: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v15/ross11a.html
- Code: no official release. Canonical maintained implementation: `algorithms.dagger` in https://github.com/HumanCompatibleAI/imitation (docs https://imitation.readthedocs.io/en/latest/).
- Mechanism: iterate — roll out the current learner π_i to induce state distribution d_{π_i}, query the expert π\* at **every visited state**, append (s, π\*(s)) to an aggregate dataset D, retrain on all of D. A mixing schedule β_i (β_1=1, β_i→0) blends expert and learner during rollout. Framed as Follow-the-Leader on an online convex problem, giving regret bounds that convert compounding error from O(T²ε) (behavior cloning) to O(Tε).

**SafeDAgger** — *Query-Efficient Imitation Learning for End-to-End Autonomous Driving*, Zhang & Cho, AAAI 2017.
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.06450 · ar5iv: https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1605.06450
- Code: no official release.
- Mechanism: trains an auxiliary **safety/doubt classifier** that predicts whether the primary policy's action will deviate from the reference beyond a threshold τ, *without* querying the reference. The reference is invoked only when the safety net fires, cutting expert-query cost by a large factor on TORCS while preserving DAgger's on-policy state coverage.

**DropoutDAgger** — Menda, Driggs-Campbell, Kochenderfer, 2017.
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.06166
- Mechanism: MC-dropout over the novice net approximates a posterior over actions; hand control to the expert when the novice's predictive variance is high. Direct precursor to EnsembleDAgger.

**EnsembleDAgger** — *A Bayesian Approach to Safe Imitation Learning*, Menda, Driggs-Campbell, Kochenderfer, IROS 2019.
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.08364
- Code: no official release.
- Mechanism: replaces the GP/dropout uncertainty proxy with an **ensemble of NNs**; the ensemble variance is a doubt score. The gating rule is a conjunction of two tests — (i) novice-expert action *discrepancy* below a bound, and (ii) ensemble variance below a bound — so control is ceded to the expert both when the novice is uncertain and when it is confidently wrong. Evaluated on inverted pendulum and MuJoCo HalfCheetah.

**HG-DAgger** — *Interactive Imitation Learning with Human Experts*, Kelly, Sidrane, Driggs-Campbell, Kochenderfer, ICRA 2019.
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.02890
- Code: no official release. Third-party: https://github.com/ErikBavenstrand/Performance-Evaluation-of-Imitation-Learning-Algorithms-with-Human-Experts ; a LeRobot fork with HG-DAgger + HIL-SERL: https://github.com/nuomizai/lerobot
- Mechanism: **human-gated** rather than stochastically-mixed. The novice drives continuously; a human supervisor watching in real time seizes full control when behavior looks unsafe, drives back to safety, and hands control back manually. Only the human-intervention segments are aggregated as labels. Also learns a safety threshold on a model-uncertainty risk metric that predicts where the trained novice will be reliable. Removes DAgger's pathological β-mixing, which is unusable with a real human driving a real vehicle.

**LazyDAgger** — *Reducing Context Switching in Interactive Imitation Learning*, Hoque, Balakrishna, Putterman, Luo, Brown, Seita, Thananjeyan, Novoseller, Goldberg, IEEE CASE 2021.
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.00053 · HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2104.00053
- Code: no separate official repo found (Berkeley AUTOLab; the ThriftyDAgger repo is the successor codebase).
- Mechanism: SafeDAgger with **asymmetric switching thresholds (hysteresis)**. Entering supervisor mode requires discrepancy > τ_up; returning to autonomy requires falling below τ_down < τ_up, and interventions additionally inject noise for robustness. Reduces context switches ~60% vs SafeDAgger on 3 continuous-control tasks and on an ABB YuMi, with ~60% higher success.

**ThriftyDAgger** — *Budget-Aware Novelty and Risk Gating for Interactive Imitation Learning*, Hoque, Balakrishna, Novoseller, Wilcox, Brown, Goldberg, CoRL 2021.
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.08273 · PMLR: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v164/hoque22a/hoque22a.pdf
- Code: **yes** — https://github.com/ryanhoque/thriftydagger
- Mechanism: a learned switching policy solicits human control at states that are either **novel** (estimated by a state-novelty / ensemble-disagreement measure — no reference behavior to imitate) or **risky** (estimated by a learned Q_risk giving probability of task failure from that state). Thresholds are calibrated to hit a *target intervention budget*, making human effort an explicit constraint rather than an emergent quantity.

**Fleet-DAgger** — *Interactive Robot Fleet Learning with Scalable Human Supervision*, Hoque et al., CoRL 2022.
- PMLR: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v205/hoque23a/hoque23a.pdf
- Code: https://github.com/ryanhoque/physical-IFL (Fleet-DAgger on 4 ABB YuMi arms, ROS2)
- Mechanism: generalizes robot-gated IIL to N robots and M < N humans; a priority function ranks which robots get the scarce human attention.

**DART** (the off-policy alternative worth citing as contrast) — *Noise Injection for Robust Imitation Learning*, Laskey, Lee, Fox, Dragan, Goldberg, CoRL 2017.
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.09327
- Mechanism: instead of querying the expert on-policy, inject optimized noise into the *supervisor* during demonstration so the supervisor demonstrates recovery. Approximates DAgger's benefit with a single off-policy collection pass — relevant precisely because it does **not** require a queryable expert.

### 1.3 **CRITICAL ANALYSIS: Is DAgger applicable when the "expert" is a noisy kinematic reference trajectory?**

**Short answer: not directly, and the literature reflects this — there is essentially no work that runs DAgger with a raw retargeted kinematic trajectory as the queryable oracle.** Targeted arXiv full-text queries for `abs:"DAgger" AND abs:"reference motion"` return almost nothing in this sense; every DAgger hit in the humanoid/character literature is *teacher–student distillation from a privileged RL policy*, not from a kinematic reference.

**Why it breaks — three separate failures of DAgger's preconditions:**

1. **The reference is not a policy.** DAgger's aggregation step is `D ← D ∪ {(s, π\*(s))} for s ~ d_{π_i}`. It requires π\*: S → A evaluable at *arbitrary* learner-visited states. A retargeted trajectory q̂_{1:T} is a **time-indexed open-loop signal**, defined only at (t), not at s. Once the simulated humanoid drifts off the reference (which is the only regime where DAgger's correction is worth anything), q̂_t is the expert's action *at a state the expert is not in* — precisely the pathology DAgger exists to fix, and the reference contains no information to fix it.

2. **Wrong action space.** q̂ lives in configuration space; the learner's action is torque or a PD target. Converting pose → action needs an inverse-dynamics model or a tracking controller, which the reference does not supply. Any "label" you manufacture is your controller's output, not the expert's.

3. **The expert is neither optimal nor feasible in the learner's MDP.** DAgger's O(Tε) bound assumes small ε = expert-class error under the learner's distribution. A retargeted human motion is generally **dynamically infeasible** for the target embodiment (different mass distribution, actuator/torque limits, contact model, foot geometry), so the relabeling target is not realizable. Empirically this is well documented: *Retargeting Matters* (Araujo, Ze, Xu, Wu, Liu, https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02252, code https://github.com/YanjieZe/GMR, site https://jaraujo98.github.io/retargeting_matters) reports that retargeting artifacts — foot sliding, self-penetration, physically infeasible poses — "significantly reduce policy robustness, particularly for dynamic or long sequences." *RobotDancing* (https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.20717) states it plainly: "retargeted reference motions are typically kinematically plausible but **dynamically inconsistent** with the robot."

**What the field actually does instead — four patterns:**

**(A) Replace the trajectory with a closed-loop *kinematic policy* conditioned on the simulator state. This is the genuine DAgger analogue, and it is exactly KinPoly and EmbodiedPose.**
KinPoly's "dynamics-regulated training" (https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2106.05969, Algorithm 2) feeds the kinematic policy the **simulated** pose q_t rather than the auto-regressive kinematic pose q̃_t: `c_t ← T_AC(q_t, õ_{t+1}, h̃_{t+1}/φ_{t+1})`, i.e., agent-centric features computed from the physics state. The policy is then supervised by the original MoCap ground truth. On-policy state distribution + off-policy expert labels — structurally identical to DAgger. The authors describe it as "a powerful data augmentation step, exposing π_KIN^step with diverse states collected from simulation" and **never cite DAgger**, but that is what it is. Same for EmbodiedPose (Algorithm 1, below in Topic 2). This is the single most important precedent for your question.

**(B) Convert the reference into a reward and run RL** (DeepMimic lineage). Reference State Initialization + early termination substitute for DAgger's on-policy correction. PHC, PhysHOI, ASAP stage-1 all do this.

**(C) Make the reference a *nominal controller* and learn a residual on top** — see Topic 3. The reference is never imitated as an expert; it is a feedforward term.

**(D) DAgger *is* used in humanoid RL — but always between two learned policies.** A privileged, full-state RL teacher is queryable at arbitrary states, so the precondition holds:
- *VIRAL* (He, Wang, Xue, Ben, Luo, Yuan, Da, … Zhu) https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.15200 — privileged RL teacher with a delta action space + RSI; vision student distilled via "a mixture of online DAgger and behavior cloning."
- *FADA* https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28476 — oracle policy with privileged info → Planner-IDM student via DAgger.
- *Perceptive Humanoid Parkour* https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15827 — DAgger distills motion-tracking RL experts into a depth-based multi-skill student.
- *LessMimic* https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.21723 — "DAgger-style distillation" aligning distance-field latents with egocentric observations.
- *TrajBooster* https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.11839 — "heuristic-enhanced harmonized online DAgger" lifting low-dim trajectory references into whole-body actions.
- *GBC* (Yao et al., https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.09960, code https://github.com/sjtu-mvasl-robotics/rsl_rl) — the closest to your question and instructive: the DAgger expert π_D is **not** the retargeted motion. Stage 1 trains π_D on retargeted data in a *simplified fixed-base* environment; Stage 2 uses π_D as the oracle, queried at current sim states, `â^Dagg ← π_Dagg(o, ô)`, with `L_CLIP(θ) ← L_CLIP(θ) + w_im · L_KL(θ)` (Eq. 6) and a LoRA-updated teacher loss `L_DAgger = (1−ρ)‖â_t^Dagg − a_t^Actor‖²` (Eq. 7). They built a queryable policy *first* precisely because the reference isn't one.

**The one route that would give you a legitimate DAgger oracle from a kinematic reference: re-solve it with a sampling MPC, which *is* state-queryable.**
- *SPIDER: Scalable Physics-Informed Dexterous Retargeting*, Pan, Wang, Qi, Liu, Bharadhwaj, Sharma, Wu, Shi, Malik, Hogan — https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.09484, site https://jc-bao.github.io/spider-project/. "Human demonstrations should provide global task structure and objective, while large-scale physics-based sampling with curriculum-style virtual contact guidance should refine trajectories to ensure dynamical feasibility." +18% success over naive sampling, 10× faster than RL, 2.4M-frame dataset.
- *Direct Dynamic Retargeting (DDR)*, Roux, De Matteïs, Jordana, Guillet, Mansard, Stasse, Souères, 2026-05 — https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23762. Single-stage sampling-based MPC inside a physics simulator, task-space formulation, "natively optimizes over complex contact sequences while mitigating input drift"; code promised, not yet posted.

**Bottom line for a system design:** if you want DAgger-style relabeling against a noisy kinematic reference, you must first promote the reference into a state-conditioned expert — either (i) a learned kinematic policy fed the simulator state (KinPoly / EmbodiedPose), or (ii) an MPC/trajectory optimizer that can be re-solved from any state (SPIDER / DDR). Feeding q̂_t directly as a label at off-reference states is behavior cloning with a mislabeled dataset, not DAgger, and its error compounds in exactly the O(T²ε) way DAgger was written to avoid.

## TOPIC 2 — Two-stage kinematic-policy + physics-correction architectures

**RFC — *Residual Force Control for Agile Human Behavior Imitation and Extended Motion Synthesis***, Yuan & Kitani, NeurIPS 2020.
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.07364 · Code: **https://github.com/Khrylx/RFC** · Site: https://www.ye-yuan.com/rfc
- Mechanism: augments the humanoid action space with an **external residual force/torque applied at the root** (explicit RFC) or as extra generalized forces (implicit RFC), compensating the dynamics mismatch between the simplified humanoid model and the real human that generated the MoCap. Enables ballet-level agility (pirouette, arabesque, jeté). Also introduces a dual-policy framework (kinematic policy + RFC-augmented dynamics policy) and is the first method to learn from Human3.6M-scale data.

**SimPoE — *Simulated Character Control for 3D Human Pose Estimation***, Yuan, Wei, Simon, Kitani, Saragih, CVPR 2021 (Oral).
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.00683 · Site: https://www.ye-yuan.com/simpoe/ · Code: **none released**
- Mechanism: a single RL policy takes (current-frame pose estimate, next image frame) and outputs the next-frame pose by controlling a simulated character. Internally it contains a **learnable kinematic pose refinement unit** that iteratively refines the kinematic estimate against 2D keypoints, then computes PD targets/torques from that refined pose. Adds a **meta-PD control** mechanism that predicts per-state PD gains. Key architectural point: the kinematic refinement is *inside* the policy, so kinematics and dynamics are trained jointly rather than stacked.

**KinPoly — *Dynamics-Regulated Kinematic Policy for Egocentric Pose Estimation***, Luo, Hachiuma, Yuan, Kitani, NeurIPS 2021.
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.05969 · ar5iv: https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2106.05969 · Code: **https://github.com/KlabCMU/KinPoly** (also https://github.com/ZhengyiLuo/UHC)
- Mechanism: kinematic policy π_KIN(video, sim state) → target pose q̃_{t+1}; a **pre-trained Universal Humanoid Controller (UHC)** tries to realize q̃_{t+1} in MuJoCo; the misalignment between instructed and achieved pose is a training signal back into π_KIN. The dynamics-regulated loop (Alg. 2) queries π_KIN on **simulated** states q_t rather than kinematically-propagated ones. Losses: supervised L_SL on root pos/rot, object pos/rot, joint angles (Eq. 2), plus RL rewards (Eq. 3) combining motion-imitation terms (w_hp, w_hq) and **dynamics self-supervision terms** (w_jr^dyna, w_jp^dyna) pushing kinematic predictions toward what the simulator actually produced. Important caveat the paper itself reports: the dynamics-regulated variant has *slightly higher* pose error than kinematics-only, but is what makes physically-valid HOI possible.
- **UHC uses RFC.** The repo README says outright: "It relies heavily on residual force control for keeping the humanoid stable, and we are actively working on relying less on this dependency," and ships both "Implicit RFC" and "Explicit RFC" checkpoints.

**EmbodiedPose — *Embodied Scene-aware Human Pose Estimation***, Luo, Iwase, Yuan, Kitani, NeurIPS 2022.
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.09106 · HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2206.09106v2 · OpenReview: https://openreview.net/forum?id=xl39QEYiB-j · Code: **https://github.com/ZhengyiLuo/EmbodiedPose**
- Mechanism (verified from the HTML): kinematic policy is an RNN predicting **velocity residuals**, `q̇̃_t = π_KIN(s_t, φ_t, o_t)`, `q̃_{t+1} = q̇̃_t + q̃_t`, where s_t is proprioception, φ_t is the **multi-step projection gradient** (a movement cue defined in the global frame from 2D keypoints, which disentangles camera pose), o_t is scene occupancy from environmental sensors. The UHC converts q̃_{t+1} to torques: `a_t ← π_UHC(a_t | s_t, q̃_{t+1})`, then `s_{t+1} ← T(s_{t+1}|s_t, a_t)`. Dynamics-regulated training again: "Using q_{t+1} verifies that q̃_t can be followed by our controller and informs our π_KIN of the current humanoid state." Kinematic loss is `L = Σ w_L1‖q̂_t − q̃_t‖₁ + w_prior D_KL(q_φ(z_t|q̃_t, q̃_{t−1}) ‖ p_θ(z_t|q̃_{t−1}))` with a HuMoR motion-prior KL term (w_L1=5, w_prior=1e-4). One-stage, causal, recovers global pose. No residual-force output from the kinematic side.

**PhysCap — *Physically Plausible Monocular 3D Motion Capture in Real Time***, Shimada, Golyanik, Xu, Theobalt, SIGGRAPH Asia 2020.
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.08880 · Code: **https://github.com/soshishimada/PhysCap_demo_release** (~213★). Successor Neural PhysCap (SIGGRAPH 2021): https://github.com/soshishimada/Neural_Physcap_Demo
- Mechanism: CNN infers joint positions → IK gives a kinematic pose → that pose becomes a **constraint** in a real-time physics-based pose optimizer accounting for gravity, floor contact, and biophysical limits. 25 fps, monocular RGB. This is the canonical *optimization*-based (not RL) physics-correction stage.

**PhysDiff — *Physics-Guided Human Motion Diffusion Model***, Yuan, Song, Iqbal, Vahdat, Kautz, ICCV 2023 (Oral), NVIDIA.
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.02500 · Site: https://nvlabs.github.io/PhysDiff/ · Code: **not released**
- Mechanism: inserts a **physics-based motion projection module** into selected reverse-diffusion steps. At those steps the denoised motion is handed to a motion-imitation policy in a physics simulator; the simulated (physically realizable) result replaces the denoised sample and seeds the next diffusion step. "The use of physics in our model iteratively pulls the motion toward a physically-plausible space." >78% improvement in physical-plausibility metrics on all datasets. Conceptually a projected-gradient / alternating-projection scheme, not DAgger.

**PhysicsVAE / *Physics-based Character Controllers Using Conditional VAEs***, Won, Gopinath, Hodgins, SIGGRAPH 2022 (ACM TOG 41(4)).
- PDF: https://research.facebook.com/publications/physics-based-character-controllers-using-conditional-vaes/ (direct: https://research.facebook.com/file/1041294433431257/Physics-based-Character-Controllers-Using-Conditional-VAEs.pdf)
- Code: **no official release found.**
- Mechanism: a conditional VAE over the *action* distribution conditioned on the character state, trained on MoCap databases, so that sampling the latent produces diverse, motion-like, physically executable behaviors. Yields a reusable low-level controller that generates long unconditioned sequences without task reward, and serves as a latent action space for downstream task policies. Directly comparable to ASE/PULSE-style skill embeddings.

### 2.1 — 2023–2026: physics policies used to CLEAN UP a noisy kinematic reference

This is the most active slice and the closest analogue to what you're describing.

**PHC — *Perpetual Humanoid Control for Real-time Simulated Avatars***, Luo, Cao, Winkler, Kitani, Xu, ICCV 2023.
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.06456 · HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2305.06456v3 · Site: https://zhengyiluo.github.io/PHC/ · Code: **https://github.com/ZhengyiLuo/PHC**, MuJoCo port **https://github.com/ZhengyiLuo/PHC_MJX**
- Designed explicitly for *noisy* kinematic input ("pose estimates from video or generated from language"). **PMCP** (progressive multiplicative control policy) allocates new primitive subnetworks to progressively harder motions with lateral connections / weight-sharing init, avoiding catastrophic forgetting. Training uses **hard-negative mining**: evaluate the current model over the whole dataset, collect the failures, train the next primitive exclusively on that subset. Robustness to noisy references comes from (i) optionally consuming only 3D joint positions (MeTRAbs) instead of noisier joint rotations, (ii) goal-conditioning on pose *differences* rather than absolute reference rotations, (iii) removing ankle/toe joints from the termination condition. ~90% success on video-estimated poses vs 18–58% for baselines; 98.9% of AMASS **with no external forces**.

**PhysPT — *Physics-aware Pretrained Transformer for Estimating Human Dynamics from Monocular Videos***, Zhang, Kephart, Cui, Ji, CVPR 2024.
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.04430 · Code: not located
- Mechanism: a Transformer encoder-decoder pretrained with a physics-based body representation and contact-force model, trained with **force loss, contact loss, and Euler-Lagrange loss**. Applied *post hoc* to any kinematics-based estimate, it improves physical plausibility and additionally outputs motion forces. Pure learned-dynamics correction — no simulator in the loop.

**Plug-and-Play Physical Motion Restoration for In-the-Wild High-Difficulty Motions**, Zhang, Li, Zhang, Pan, Wang, Liu, Li, 2024-12-23.
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.17377
- Mechanism: two modules. **MCM** (mask-based motion correction) uses video segmentation masks + motion context to repair flawed frames, producing "imitation-friendly" motion — i.e., it denoises the reference *before* the physics stage precisely because the imitator cannot track a broken reference. **PTM** (physics-based motion transfer) uses a pretrain-then-adapt scheme for imitation of in-the-wild high-difficulty motion. Explicitly framed as a plug-and-play refinement layer over existing video mocap.

**Physics-Guided Human Motion Capture with Pose Probability Modeling**, Ju, Huang, Zhu, Li, Wang, IJCAI 2023.
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.09910 · Code: **https://github.com/Me-Ditto/Physics-Guided-Mocap**
- The clearest problem statement in the literature for your question: "Existing solutions always adopt kinematic results as reference motions, and the physics is treated as a post-processing module. However, due to the depth ambiguity, monocular motion capture inevitably suffers from noises, and **the noisy reference often leads to failure for physics-based tracking**." Their fix: model the 2D-to-3D lifting uncertainty with a latent Gaussian, sample references from that distribution, track each with a physics module, and feed the tracked-vs-image discrepancy back as explicit guidance into the reverse diffusion — "physics-based tracking and kinematic denoising promote each other."

**PhysHMR — *Learning Humanoid Control Policies from Vision for Physically Plausible Human Motion Reconstruction***, Feng, Huang, Wang, Gu, Liu, 2025-10-02.
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02566 · Code: not located
- Mechanism: abolishes the two-stage design — learns a **visual-to-action policy** directly. "Pixel-as-ray" lifts 2D keypoints into 3D spatial rays in global space and feeds them as policy input, sidestepping noisy 3D root predictions. Includes a **distillation scheme transferring motion knowledge from a mocap-trained expert to the vision-conditioned policy**, then refines with physics-motivated RL rewards. Direct critique of the KinPoly/PhysCap stack: "this two-stage design introduces error accumulation."

**PhysDynPose / MoviCam — *Physics-based Human Pose Estimation from a Single Moving RGB Camera***, Aytekin, Li, Luvizon, Dabral, Oswald, Habermann, Theobalt, 2025-07.
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.17406
- Handles the non-flat-ground / moving-camera case that breaks most physics trackers; introduces the first non-synthetic dataset with GT moving-camera trajectories + scene geometry + contact labels.

**RePHO — *Recovering Physically Plausible Human-Object Interactions from Monocular Videos***, Huang, Vouga, Huang, Pavlakos, CVPR 2026.
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05359 · Site: https://dingbang777.github.io/RePHO/
- **Most directly on-point 2026 work.** Starts from a kinematic estimate, refines by training an RL policy to reproduce the interaction in simulation. Critically: "Because kinematic estimates are typically noisy, **naive RL training can fail**. Therefore, we propose an adaptive sampling strategy with a **dual self-updating mechanism** that can identify the frames with the most informative and reliable kinematic reconstruction." That is the noisy-expert problem solved by *selective trust in the reference* — a confidence-gated relabeling, structurally the closest thing in this literature to DAgger's gating logic, applied to reference quality rather than to a human supervisor.

**CRISP — *Contact-Guided Real2Sim from Monocular Video with Planar Scene Primitives***, Wang, Wang, Tan, Zhao, Hodgins, Tulsiani, Ramanan, ICLR 2026.
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.14696 · Site: https://crisp-real2sim.github.io/CRISP-Real2Sim/
- Attacks the *scene* half of the noise problem — noisy reconstructed geometry is what makes tracking policies fail. Fits planar primitives to get simulation-ready convex geometry; cuts motion-tracking failure from 55.2% → 6.9%.

**Mimic2DM — *Learning to Control Physically-simulated 3D Characters via Generating and Mimicking 2D Motions***, Li, Chen, Huang, Wong, 2025-12.
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.08500
- Skips 3D reconstruction entirely: trains a single-view 2D motion-tracking policy by minimizing reprojection error, avoiding the noisy-3D-reference problem at the source.

**Real2Sim in HOI / HA-HOI** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14462 (2026-05) — human-first/object-follow reconstruction producing references that actually hold contact when used for humanoid-object simulation.

**PhysHOI — *Physics-Based Imitation of Dynamic Human-Object Interaction***, Wang, Lin, Zeng, Luo, Zhang, Zhang, 2023-12.
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.04393 · Code: **https://github.com/wyhuai/PhysHOI** (BallPlay dataset, Isaac Gym, 51×3 DoF whole-body humanoid with dexterous hands; built on ASE + UHC)
- Mechanism: imitates HOI from **kinematic demonstrations** with no task-specific reward engineering, using an explicit **contact graph** over body-part↔object relations and a matching contact reward. Note for your purposes: PhysHOI is a *tracking* method over a fixed kinematic HOI reference; it does not do iterative kinematic-policy refinement the way KinPoly/EmbodiedPose do — it is pattern (B), reference-as-reward.

**Skeleton2Humanoid** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.04294 (ACM MM 2022) — three stages: test-time adaptation of the synthesis net to improve physical plausibility of skeleton joints, analytical IK to humanoid, then **curriculum residual force control** motion imitation. A useful data point that RFC keeps being reached for when the reference is noisy.

**Universal Humanoid Motion Representations (PULSE)**, Luo, Cao, Merel, Winkler, Huang, Kitani, Xu, ICLR 2024 Spotlight — https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.04582 — distills PHC into a VAE latent with an information bottleneck; PULSE uses the PHC+ checkpoint (see PHC repo).

## TOPIC 3 — Residual RL formulations

### 3.1 Foundations

**Residual Policy Learning**, Silver, Allen, Tenenbaum, Kaelbling, 2018-12.
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.06298 · Code: **https://github.com/k-r-allen/residual-policy-learning** (~85★, MuJoCo envs `rpl_environments` + TF impl, baselines-based)
- Mechanism: given an imperfect, possibly nondifferentiable initial controller π₀ (hand-designed, or an MPC), learn `π(s) = π₀(s) + f_θ(s)` with model-free deep RL, only f_θ trained. Evaluated on six MuJoCo manipulation tasks stressing partial observability, sensor noise, model misspecification, and controller miscalibration (SlipperyPush, FetchHook, TwoFrameHookNoisy, ComplexHook). Key claim: solves long-horizon sparse-reward problems neither RL alone nor the controller alone can, and **never does worse than π₀**.

**Residual Reinforcement Learning for Robot Control**, Johannink, Bahl, Nair, Luo, Kumar, Loskyll, Aparicio Ojea, Solowjow, Levine, ICRA 2019.
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.03201
- Mechanism: decompose control into a conventional feedback part (handles the smooth, well-modeled dynamics) and an RL part (handles contact, friction, and other hard-to-model effects); sum the two signals. Real-world block assembly with contacts and unstable objects. Same algebra as RPL, different motivation — the split is *by physical regime* rather than *by controller imperfection*.

### 3.2 Residual action over a retargeted / PD reference in humanoid RL (2024–2026)

**I-CTRL — *Imitation to Control Humanoid Robots Through Constrained Reinforcement Learning***, Yan, Valls Mascaro, Egle, Lee, 2024-05 (v2 2025-02).
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.08726 · HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2405.08726v2 · Site: https://evm7.github.io/I-CTRL/ · Code: not located on GitHub
- **The cleanest formalization of exactly your setup.** Residual action added directly to the retargeted reference joint positions: **q_t^des = q_t^ref + a_t**, with the action specifying PD targets. The "bounded" part is a hard clip in joint space: `‖q_t^des − q_t^ref‖ ≤ δ`, with **δ = 10° for arms, δ = 30° for legs and torso**. Framed explicitly as "a constrained refinement over non-physics-based retargeted motions." Generalizes across five robots with a single simple reward; includes an automatic priority scheduler for large motion datasets.

**RobotDancing — *Residual-Action RL Enables Robust Long-Horizon Humanoid Motion Tracking***, Sun, Peng, Meng, Li, Huang, Bing, Wang, Knoll, RA-L (v2 2026-08).
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.20717
- Single-stage recipe predicting **reference-conditioned residual joint targets**. Rationale stated directly: "By parameterizing actions as residual corrections on top of the reference, the policy can focus on compensating reference–robot dynamics mismatch (actuation limits, latency, friction, inertia) rather than re-synthesizing the motion." Eight LAFAN1 dance motions on Unitree G1; cross-platform on H1 and H1-2.

**RuN — *Residual Policy for Natural Humanoid Locomotion***, Li, Zhu, Wu, Yuan, Zhang, Yang, Liu, 2025-09.
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.20696
- Decoupled residual learning: a pre-trained **Conditional Motion Generator** supplies a kinematically natural motion prior; the RL policy learns only a lightweight residual handling dynamical interaction. Unitree G1, 0–2.5 m/s including smooth walk→run transitions. The explicit argument against monolithic tracking: "Existing DRL methods typically require the policy to directly track a reference motion, forcing a single policy to simultaneously learn motion imitation, velocity tracking, and stability maintenance."

**HDMI — *Learning Interactive Humanoid Whole-Body Control from Human Videos***, Weng, Li, Sobanbabu, Wang, Luo, He, Ramanan, Shi, 2025-09.
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.16757 · Site: https://hdmi-humanoid.github.io/
- Three design choices called out: unified object representation, **residual action space**, general interaction reward. 67 consecutive door traversals + 6 real loco-manipulation tasks on G1.

**FAST — *General Humanoid Whole-Body Control via Pretraining and Fast Adaptation***, Wang et al., 2026-02.
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11929
- **Parseval-Guided Residual Policy Adaptation**: a lightweight *delta action policy* under orthogonality and KL constraints, for OOD motion adaptation without catastrophic forgetting. Plus Center-of-Mass-Aware Control. The orthogonality constraint is the interesting bit — it keeps the residual from re-spanning the base policy's action subspace.

**REGRIND — *A Minimalist Retargeting-Guided RL Recipe for Dexterous Manipulation***, Feng, Leung, Wang, Yang, Qi, Culbertson, 2026-07.
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11874 · Site: https://yunhaifeng.com/REGRIND
- Ports the humanoid recipe to dexterous hands: retarget → **residual RL policy tracking object-centric keypoints along the reference** → zero-shot to hardware with careful sysID. From a single human demonstration.

**ASAP — *Aligning Simulation and Real-World Physics***, He, Gao, Xiao, Zhang, Wang, Wang, Luo, He, Pan, Yi, Qu, Kitani, Hodgins, Fan, Zhu, RSS 2025.
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.01143 · Site: https://agile.human2humanoid.com/ · Code: **https://github.com/LeCAR-Lab/ASAP** (~2.1k★)
- A residual in *dynamics* rather than in action-over-reference: stage 1 pretrains tracking policies on retargeted human motion; stage 2 collects real rollouts and trains a **delta (residual) action model** that compensates sim↔real dynamics mismatch; then fine-tunes the pretrained policy inside the delta-corrected simulator. The delta action model is the residual-RL idea applied to the *simulator* — the standard 2025 alternative to sysID/DR.

Related delta-action work: *BumbleBee* (iterative delta action modeling with real data, https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.12779), *TAM: Torque Adaptation Module* (residual torque corrections between low-level controller and torque interface, CoRL 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06218, site https://dongwon-son.github.io/tam-project-page/), *DiffRacing*'s differentiable Delta Action Model (https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08019), *Perceptive BFM* (https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08059 — keeps raw kinematic references as the behavioral interface, adapts contacts/posture/timing from terrain).

### 3.3 RFC successors and the criticism of RFC

**The criticism, in the primary source.** PHC (https://arxiv.org/html/2305.06456v3) is the standard citation:

> "the external force compromises physical realism by acting as a **'hand of God'** that puppets the humanoid, leading to artifacts such as flying and floating."

and, on the mechanism, that RFC lets "the model freely apply a **non-physical force** on the humanoid," producing unnatural floating and swinging on hard sequences. PHC's counter-demonstration is the load-bearing evidence: **98.9% of AMASS with a plain PD controller and no external forces**, and ~90% success imitating video-estimated poses vs 18–58% for baselines — i.e., RFC is not necessary even in the noisy-reference regime it was most attractive for. PHC's abstract states it directly: "scales up to learning ten thousand motion clips **without using any external stabilizing forces**."

**The admission, in the RFC lineage itself.** The UHC README (https://github.com/ZhengyiLuo/UHC): "It relies heavily on residual force control for keeping the humanoid stable, and we are actively working on relying less on this dependency." UHC ships explicit-RFC and implicit-RFC variants and points forward to PHC / PHC_MJX as the successors. So the KinPoly → EmbodiedPose → UHC branch is RFC-dependent, and the PHC → PULSE branch is the deliberate RFC-free replacement.

**Why this matters for a noisy-reference design.** RFC's residual force is the *wrong* residual: it absorbs reference infeasibility into a fictitious external wrench at the root, so a noisy or dynamically impossible reference gets "tracked" by cheating rather than by correcting. The 2024–2026 residual formulations (I-CTRL, RobotDancing, RuN, FAST, REGRIND) put the residual in the **joint-target / action space with an explicit bound** instead, which keeps every force the character exerts physically realizable while still letting the policy deviate from an infeasible reference. I-CTRL's δ-clip (10°/30°) is the most explicit statement of that design principle.
