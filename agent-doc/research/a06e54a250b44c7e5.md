# Graceful degradation under reference noise — Isaac Lab / G1+Dex3 hand-object tracking

**Headline:** the literature you'd expect to solve this mostly doesn't. PHC, H2O, OmniH2O, HumanPlus, ExBody2 all *claim* robustness to noisy video pose estimates but **none of them inject noise into the reference during training** — I checked the full texts. They get robustness from dynamics randomization + fail-recovery + data filtering. The papers that actually train on corrupted references are all 2026, mostly code-free, and three of them (RGMT, CLOT, GigaBrain-WBC) are on exactly your robot.

## 1. Reference / demonstration noise injection

### 1a. Methods that genuinely corrupt the reference during training

**RGMT — Robust and Generalized Humanoid Motion Tracking**
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.23080 · project https://zeonsunlightyu.github.io/RGMT.github.io/ · **no code** · Jan 2026, arXiv only
- The closest thing in the literature to your problem statement. Unitree G1 29-DoF, Isaac Gym, 5,680 envs on one RTX 4090, 3.5 h of LAFAN1+AMASS. Architecture is the contribution: a **causal Transformer history encoder** over K=10 proprio steps (93-dim obs → 128-dim tokens, sinusoidal PE, causal mask, **max-pool over time** → dynamics embedding `h_t`), which becomes the **query** for a single **cross-attention block over a ±L=10 reference-command window** (38-dim/step). Because attention weights are conditioned on current dynamics, the encoder down-weights command tokens inconsistent with what the robot is actually doing. Asymmetric actor-critic: critic sees noise-free state, actor sees noisy obs.
- Their noise spec (Table II), scaled uniformly by a "noise level %": `Δv_ref ~ U([-0.5,0.5]³)` (z: ±0.2 m/s), `Δω_ref ~ U([-0.52,0.52]³)` rad/s, `Δg_ref ~ U([-0.05,0.05]³)`, `Δq_ref ~ U([-0.1,0.1])` rad. Baselines (GMT, Any2Track) collapse past 200%; RGMT stays stable to **1500%**. Ablation: replacing cross-attention with self-attention is the single biggest robustness loss — the cross-attention is the noise filter, not the history encoder.
- **Verdict: highest value/effort ratio of anything here.** The encoder is ~80 lines of PyTorch dropped in front of your rsl_rl actor; the noise table is a 10-line env wrapper. Do this first.

**GigaBrain-WBC-0.5 — Behavior World Model**
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18234 · **no code** · Aug 2026 (v4 Sep 2026), Unitree G1
- Encodes a **noisy, partially masked reference window** into a continuous behavior command `z`, with a **training-only FSQ (finite scalar quantizer) branch** that decodes `z` back to the *clean* reference and re-encodes for cycle-consistency — i.e. a denoising autoencoder bottleneck on the reference, with FSQ preventing latent-scale collapse. Main Transformer branch bypasses FSQ and consumes continuous `z`. It jointly predicts action, next state, and a **4-component Gaussian mixture over the next command latent** `G_{t+1}`.
- Deployment filter (this is the good part): `c_t → E → z_raw --G_t--> z* → BWM`. Pick the max-responsibility mixture component `k*`, and if the squared Mahalanobis distance `M²_{k*}(z_raw) > R²_safe` (they use `R_safe = 3`), radially retract: `z* = μ_{k*} + sqrt(R²_safe / M²_{k*}(z_raw)) · (z_raw − μ_{k*})`. Keeps the intended direction, clips the magnitude. Loss: `L_PPO + λ_recon L_recon + λ_cycle L_cycle + λ_state ||ŝ_{t+1} − s_{t+1}||² − λ_beh d_z⁻¹ log G_{t+1}(z_{t+1})`.
- **Verdict: 4-5 weeks if you already have a working tracker.** The command filter alone (without the world-model head) is ~2 days and gives you a principled "reject tier-3 garbage" mechanism. The FSQ denoising branch is the other half and is worth it.

**CLOT — Randomized Observation Pre-shift**
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15060 · project https://CLOT.github.io/ · **no code** · Feb 2026 · PNDbotics Adam Pro
- Dead simple and exactly the asymmetry you want: with some probability, shift the reference window the actor observes forward by `δ ∈ [0, T_max_pre]`, so `s̃_t^actor = [g_{t+1+δ : t+10+δ}, s_t^prop, s_{t-10:t-1}^hist]`, **but compute the tracking reward against the un-shifted reference at time t**. Disabled at inference. Curriculum-scheduled (pre-shift range 1→2, rate 5e-6). Gives smooth implicit interpolation across reference gaps/discontinuities instead of the aggressive error-minimizing snaps you get from initial-state perturbation.
- Full DR table and curriculum params are in the appendix (friction [0.8,1.5], RFI limit scale, push interval, noise curriculum 0.05→1.0).
- **Verdict: 1-2 days.** Temporal desync is a major failure mode of monocular reconstruction, and nothing else in the literature addresses it directly. Cheap experiment, do it week 1.

**OmniTrack — General Motion Tracking via Physics-Consistent Reference**
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23832 · project https://omnitrack-humanoid.github.io/ · **no code** · Feb 2026
- Two-stage reference repair. Stage I: a privileged policy (full global state, contacts, loose early-termination threshold) rolls out the *raw noisy* reference in sim; **"we do not apply domain randomization in this stage. Instead, we inject noise into motion commands"**; the resulting rollout states are recorded as the new, physically-feasible reference (incl. per-link contact masks). Stage II: train the deployable proprio-only tracker against those cleaned references, now with full DR + obs noise. Removes the fidelity-vs-feasibility conflict before the hard learning problem starts.
- **Verdict: 2-3 weeks, high payoff for your tier-2/tier-3 references.** This is the cleanest published answer to "my reference is physically impossible." Same family as H2O's "sim-to-data" and ASAP.

**CLONE — velocity-dependent SDE noise on the reference head pose**
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08931 · project https://humanoid-clone.github.io/ · June 2025 · Unitree G1
- The only teleop paper that models reference error with a *physically-motivated* process rather than iid Gaussian. To match LiDAR odometry error characteristics: `dP_head = ṗ_head dt + (||ṗ_head||/c_vel + c_min) dW`, where `W` is a Wiener process — diffusion scales with reference speed, so fast motion gets more reference error, which is exactly how monocular reconstruction behaves. Other body targets are recomputed by FK from the randomized head; they periodically reset and cap max deviation to avoid unphysical drift.
- **Verdict: half a day to port, and it's a better noise model than Gaussian for your tier-3 data.** Speed-scaled diffusion + FK propagation preserves kinematic consistency, which iid per-joint noise destroys.

**Heracles — asymmetric noisy-*state* augmentation (contrast case)**
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.27756 · **no code** · Mar 2026
- The *mirror image* of your setup: corrupt only proprioception with channel-wise Gaussian noise (`p̃_t = p_t + ε`, reduced magnitude on root pose/orientation), keep the reference clean. Ablation is striking: removing it drops completion rate 90.6% → 78.6%, joint error +42%, **height error +91%**. Useful because it quantifies how much an asymmetric noise channel is worth, and because your real deployment has *both* channels noisy.
- **Verdict: 1 day, and you should run both directions.**

**TWIST — large end-effector perturbations**
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.02833 · https://yanjieze.com/TWIST/ · 2025
- Not reference noise, but relevant: training with large EE force perturbations was necessary to stop drift/jitter during stationary contact-rich poses. Without it, policies drift at stationary poses. Given Dex3 contact, worth including.

### 1c. Hand-object-specific reference augmentation (most relevant to Dex3)

**SkillMimic-V2 — RLID from sparse and noisy demonstrations**
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.02094 · https://github.com/Ingrid789/SkillMimic-V2 (Apache-2.0, partial) · SIGGRAPH 2025 · Isaac Gym
- Directly targets "demonstration noise and coverage limitations" in human-object interaction. Three parts: **Stitched Trajectory Graph** (discovers feasible transitions between demo skills), **State Transition Field** (connects arbitrary states in the demo neighborhood back to the manifold), **Adaptive Trajectory Sampling** + a history encoder pre-trained to predict future states from motion history. The premise is that infinite physically feasible trajectories bridge noisy sparse demos.
- **Verdict: STF is the transferable idea (3-4 weeks). STG needs a graph build over your dataset — skip unless you have many clips.**

**InterReal — HOI motion augmentation with contact constraints**
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07516 · **no code** · Mar 2026 · Isaac Gym
- Their empirical finding matches your problem exactly: *"sensor disturbances in human and object trajectories, particularly **relative position perturbations between them**, can easily drive the learned policy out of distribution or even cause collapse."* Fix: offset the object by `Δp_xy^j = [Δx, Δy, 0]`, `Δx, Δy ∈ linspace(−ε, ε, √c₃)`, then **re-solve IK for both arms** in the pelvis frame so hand-object contact stays consistent with the anchor motion. Trains one policy on the resulting multi-motion set. Plus an outer-loop meta-learner for automatic reward weighting.
- **Verdict: 1-2 weeks, and the highest-relevance augmentation for object-scored tracking.**

**A Minimalist Retargeting-Guided RL Recipe for Dexterous Manipulation**
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11874 · **no code** · Jul 2026
- Cheaper version of the same idea, no IK re-solve. Perturb initial object pose (±5 cm position, ±30° yaw) → rigid `ΔT₀ ∈ SE(3)`, then apply a **time-varying warp `ΔT(t)` interpolating from `ΔT₀` at t=0 to identity at the goal**, to *both* the object pose and the hand root pose at every step. Relative spatial/contact structure is preserved exactly, and you generate unlimited augmented references at training time with zero optimization. Scored on object keypoint tracking error.
- **Verdict: 2-3 days. Do this.** It's the single cheapest way to broaden your reference distribution without breaking contact.

**DexMan — contact-prior attraction reward for noisy hand-object poses**
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.08475 · https://github.com/EmbodiedAI-NTU/DexMan · Oct 2025
- Names the dilemma precisely: with noisy single-view references, strict imitation pushes toward infeasible behavior, relaxed imitation collapses to a contact-avoiding local minimum. Fix: offline, for each hand keypoint `j` at time `t`, find the nearest object mesh vertex `v^o_{j,t} = argmin_v ||p^hum_{j,t} − v||`, keep pairs within threshold `τ_j` as contact candidates `P(t)`; then reward the *robot* for realizing that keypoint↔vertex correspondence **in the object frame**. Absolute world-space hand trajectory is abandoned as the objective. Also: they pre-settle object poses by perturbing with 20 random rotations (±45°) and selecting the stable one closest to the original.
- **Verdict: 1 week, and it's the right reward structure for object-scored evaluation.** Object-frame targets are invariant to exactly the errors monocular reconstruction makes.

**DexTrack** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.09614 · https://github.com/Meowuu7/DexTrack · ICLR 2025 · Isaac Gym, GRAB+TACO. Gives you a **formal reference-quality metric** (see §2). Reward weights are informative for your scoring: `w_obj,pos = 1.0`, `w_obj,rot = 0.3`, `w_hand,wrist = 0.1`, `w_hand,finger = 0.1` — object position gets 10× the hand terms. Homotopy optimization and the IL+RL combination are **not released**.

Others worth knowing: **InterMimic** https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.20390 · https://github.com/Sirui-Xu/InterMimic (CVPR 2025 Highlight, **Isaac Gym + Isaac Lab, SMPL-X and Unitree G1**, teacher-student with retargeting+correction); **Dexplore** https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.09671 (sparse goals by selectively unmasking reference components, episode-fixed latent noise ε for temporal consistency); **DexMachina** https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.24853; **ConTrack** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03177 (adaptive task-style mixing + mid-trajectory reset library + contact priors); **OmniRetarget** https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.26633 (interaction-mesh retargeting with hard constraints, G1, one demo → many terrains/object configs); **TopoRetarget** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16272; **Omnigrasp** https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.11385.

## 2. Noise-conditioned / uncertainty-aware policies

There is no humanoid paper that conditions on a scalar "reference quality." But two papers hand you all the parts.

**StableHand — Quality-Aware Flow Matching (the quality-estimator blueprint)**
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18553 · project https://huajian-zeng.github.io/projects/stablehand · **no code** · May 2026
- Per-frame, **per-component** quality `q_t = [q^L_W, q^L_F, q^R_W, q^R_F] ∈ [0,1]⁴` — wrist and fingers kept separate because their error scales decouple (wrist = tens of mm of global drift, fingers = mm of articulation). Ground-truth quality: `e^h_W = ||j̄₀ − j₀|| + r₀·arccos[(tr(R̄ᵀR) − 1)/2]` with MANO palm radius `r₀ = 85 mm`; `e^h_F` = MPJPE in the GT wrist frame after alignment rotation `R(R̄)ᵀ`. Then `q = exp(−e/σ)` with **σ calibrated so the worst 20% of frames land at q < 0.1**, i.e. `σ* = p₈₀(e)/ln 10`.
- At inference you don't have GT, so a **Quality Network** predicts the error `ê` in mm (not `q`, because mm is corpus-invariant while σ isn't): temporal self-attention with RoPE over a T-frame window of observations + binary confidence flags + camera pose, a learnable `[geo]` token for trajectory-level context, per-frame 2-layer MLP predicting `log(1 + ê)`. Trained by applying a perturbation pool to clean data. `q̂ = exp(−ê/σ̂)`.
- The quality signal then drives four things: per-channel forward noise schedule, a velocity target that **freezes high-quality channels and regenerates low-quality ones**, AdaLN modulation of the DiT, and a quality-aware ODE init.
- **Verdict: the Quality Network is ~1 week and is the missing piece for your three-tier setup.** You already have the supervision (tier-1 clean ↔ tier-2/3 corrupted pairs) — train a small causal net to regress per-frame reference error, feed `q̂` to the policy as an extra obs channel, and train with `q̂` computed on-the-fly from your synthetic corruption. That *is* a noise-conditioned policy.

**CMP — Competence Manifold Projection**
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07457 · project https://shepherd1226.github.io/CMP/ · **no code** · Apr 2026
- Frame-Wise Safety Scheme turns the infinite-horizon safety constraint into a single-step manifold-inclusion test; a **Lower-Bounded Safety Estimator** distinguishes unmastered intents from the training distribution; an **Isomorphic Latent Space** aligns manifold geometry with safety probability so projection is O(1). Up to 10× survival-rate improvement on OOD inputs, <10% tracking degradation, and it produces "best-effort" behavior instead of failing or e-stopping.
- Their **OOD-Sensor dataset generation is directly reusable as a tier-3 simulator**: at random events, add a *persistent* bias for the remainder of the trajectory — position drift `δ_p ~ N(0, 0.2²)` m, orientation drift `δ_r ~ N(0, 30²)°`. Persistent bias, not iid jitter, is what VIO/monocular failures look like.
- **Verdict: the OOD data recipe is 1 day; full CMP is 3-4 weeks.** GigaBrain's Mahalanobis retraction (§1a) is a simpler instantiation of the same idea — prefer that.

**DexTrack's reference-quality triple** (from https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.09614, appendix): three quantities computable from the reference alone, no ground truth needed —
- Smoothness `t_s(T) = (1/(N−1)) Σ_i (1/Δt)·[w_s·(s_{i+1}−s_i)/Δt − w_s·(s_i−s_{i−1})/Δt]` — second finite difference, catches jitter
- Consistency `t_c(T) = (1/(N−1)) Σ_i ||(p^o_i − p^o_{i−1})/Δt − (p^h_i − p^h_{i−1})/Δt||` — hand-wrist vs object velocity mismatch, catches broken grasps
- Penetration `t_p(T) = (1/(N+1)) Σ_i Pene_Depth(s_i, P^o)` — max hand-object penetration against the object point cloud

`s_quality = E[(t_s + t_c + t_p)/3]`, and robustness `s_r(π) = s_quality(L) / min(L_L(π), ε)`. **These three are the best candidate inputs for a noise-conditioned policy in your setting** — cheap, differentiable-free, no GT, and directly diagnostic of monocular-reconstruction failure modes. ~2 days to compute in a windowed fashion and append to obs.

**Context-aware DR / observable randomization parameters:**
- **RMA** https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.04034 · https://ashish-kmr.github.io/rma-legged-robots/ · RSS 2021 · no official code. Base policy conditioned on latent extrinsics `z_t = μ(e_t)` from privileged env params; adaptation module regresses `ẑ_t` from proprio history via supervised regression. The canonical two-phase recipe. Hundreds of reimplementations in legged-gym/Isaac Lab.
- **HoRD** https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04412 · project https://tonywang-0517.github.io/hord/ · **no code** · Feb 2026. **Trained in Isaac Lab** — the most directly comparable. History-Conditioned Dynamics Representation (RMA's successor with a Transformer) + **episode-level** DR (sample `ψ^(e)` once per episode and hold it — explicitly *not* per-step iid) + online distillation. Under test-time DR, HoRD holds 84.4% success where MaskedMimic drops below 10%, OmniH2O 70.2%, ExBody2 69.4%. Ablations: no DR → collapse; no HCDR → collapse under sim-to-sim. Also: "the student policy additionally receives mild observation noise during training."
- **UP-OSI** (Yu et al., RSS 2017, "Preparing for the Unknown") is the original explicit-SysID-conditioned policy; **Dynamics as Prompts** https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.20357 · https://sim2real-capture.github.io/ is the 2024 in-context version (adjusts sim params online from interaction history, no gradients).
- **Episode-level over per-step is a repeated finding** — HoRD says it explicitly, MaskedMimic says it about masks, BFMTrack says it about latents, Dexplore holds ε fixed per episode. Whatever noise you inject, make it temporally correlated.

## 3. Observation-noise robustness: theory and practice

**SA-MDP / SA-PPO / SA-DDPG / SA-DQN**
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.08938 · https://github.com/chenhongge/StateAdvDRL (also https://github.com/huanzhang12/SA_PPO, https://github.com/huanzhang12/SA_DDPG) · **NeurIPS 2020**
- Formalizes exactly your setting: the adversary perturbs the *observation*, not the state, so the dynamics are unchanged but the policy is misled. Key theoretical results: an optimal policy under SA-MDP may not exist in general, and the performance gap is bounded by the total-variation distance between `π(·|s)` and `π(·|ν(s))` over the perturbation set. The practical output is a **policy-smoothness regularizer** `max_{ŝ ∈ B(s)} D(π(·|s), π(·|ŝ))` added to the PPO loss, solved with convex relaxation or SGLD. They report robust policies improve nominal performance too, in several environments.
- **Verdict: the SA-PPO regularizer is ~40 lines on top of rsl_rl PPO and is the single best-justified addition if you want a principled loss term rather than just data augmentation.** Use the cheap SGLD/projected-gradient inner max, not the certified relaxation. 3-4 days including tuning `κ`.

**ATLA / PA-ATLA** https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.08452 · https://github.com/huanzhang12/ATLA_robust_RL · ICLR 2021. Alternates training the agent and a *learned optimal adversary* (formulated as its own MDP), with LSTM policies. Stronger than fixed-radius noise, ~2× the training cost. **PA-AD** https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.05087 · ICLR 2022 gives the theoretically optimal attacker (actor-director decomposition) — use it to *evaluate* your tiers, not necessarily to train.

**RADIAL-RL** https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.01976 · https://github.com/tuomaso/radial_rl_v2 · NeurIPS 2021. Adversarial loss from bound propagation rather than sampling; also introduces **Greedy Worst-Case Reward (GWC)** as an attack-agnostic robustness metric. Cheaper than ATLA.

**WocaR-RL** https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.05927 · NeurIPS 2022 — worst-case-aware robust RL *without* running attacks in the loop (estimates a lower bound on worst-case value). The efficiency answer if SA-PPO/ATLA are too slow.

**Beyond Worst-case Attacks** https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.12673 · ICLR 2024 — learns a set of non-dominated policies and adapts at test time to the *actual* attack strength. Conceptually the closest theory match to your three-tier evaluation: you don't want one worst-case-robust policy, you want a family indexed by noise level.

**Robotics applications 2024-2026:**
- **Rethinking Robustness Assessment: Adversarial Attacks on Learning-based Quadrupedal Locomotion Controllers** https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.12424 · **RSS 2024** — shows that empirically-robust legged controllers fail under principled attacks; good source of an evaluation protocol.
- **Learning H-Infinity Locomotion Control** https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.14405 — actor vs. disturber adversarial game with an H∞ constraint bounding the ratio of performance degradation to disturbance intensity. Legged-gym based, most "deployable" of the adversarial family.
- **Robust Quadrupedal Locomotion via Risk-Averse Policy Learning** https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.09405; **Policy Smoothing for Provably Robust RL** https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.11420; **Time-Constrained Robust MDPs** https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.08395; **RRLS robust RL suite** https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.08406.
- Honest assessment: **nobody has applied SA-MDP to humanoid motion tracking.** That's a publishable gap, and it's a natural framing for your paper — your three noise tiers are literally three perturbation-set radii.

**Related noisy-demonstration theory:** **DART** https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.09327 · CoRL 2017 — inject noise into the *supervisor* during demonstration so the demos cover recovery; DexTrack explicitly invokes this framing. **Robust Imitation Learning from Corrupted Demonstrations** https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.12594 — Median-of-Means objective with guarantees under a constant fraction of arbitrary outliers; relevant if your tier-3 data has catastrophic per-clip failures rather than uniform degradation. **Bi-Level Motion Imitation** below.

## 4. Test-time adaptation and online reference refinement

**BFMTrack — Latent Sequence Optimization** (the paper you named; ID verified)
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25056 · Disney Research Zurich (Rupf, Serifi, Müller, Christen, Grandia, Knoop, Bächer) · Jun 23 2026 · **no code**
- Repurposes a pretrained Behavioral Foundation Model (forward-backward, Touati & Ollivier) for time-varying objectives by optimizing a **sequence of latents** `{z_t}` at inference via simulation rollouts + a policy-gradient update. No reward engineering. The key trick: sample the latent sequence as **temporally correlated colored noise** with PSD `S(f) ∝ 1/f^β`, `β = 1` (pink) — white noise causes frequent transitions between unrelated motions, averages out over the trajectory, and kills the policy-gradient signal. Final latents = optimized means. For sparse keyframes: initialize with `B(g_{t_k})` at keyframes, SLERP between, set `r_t = 0` on intermediate steps.
- Concrete budget: **24 iterations, N = 128 parallel envs, Adam lr 6.25e-3, betas (0.8, 0.99), γ = 0.97, exploration σ = 0.0125 — a few minutes per trajectory on one RTX 5090.**
- **Verdict: LSO as published needs a pretrained BFM you don't have (that's months). But the transferable core is 3-4 days:** run the *same* optimization directly over your reference trajectory (or a low-dim spline/offset parameterization of it) using your own PPO policy's rollouts as the objective. Pink-noise exploration + 24 iterations × 128 envs is trivially affordable in Isaac Lab, and it's exactly "refine the noisy reference online using the physics policy's own rollouts."

**BRIC — Bridging Kinematic Plans and Physical Control at Test Time**
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.20431 · project https://bric2026.github.io/ · Nov 2025 (v3 Dec 2025)
- Test-time adaptation of a PPO+AMP physics controller to noisy diffusion-generated motion plans. Two mechanisms: (1) **anti-forgetting via EMA target networks** — maintain `θ'_π, θ'_V, θ'_D ← α θ' + (1−α) θ` and add `L_CF = (V(s;θ_V) − V(s;θ'_V))² + ||μ(s;θ_π) − μ(s;θ'_π)||² + (D(τ;θ_D) − D(τ;θ'_D))²` over a 10-frame segment `τ`; (2) **signal-space guidance** — instead of backpropagating through the diffusion model, update the motion directly `x₀ ← x₀ − η ∇_{x₀} L_guide(Ψ(x₀))` then resample `x_t ~ q(x_t|x₀)`. Guidance losses: collision, motion smoothness, heading consistency.
- **Verdict: the EMA-consistency loss is 1-2 days and is the safety net for any test-time adaptation you attempt.** Without it, adapting to tier-3 references will destroy your tier-1 performance — which is exactly the failure mode your scoring punishes.

**Bi-Level Motion Imitation**
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.01968 · project https://sites.google.com/view/bmi-corl2024 · **CoRL 2024**
- Alternates optimizing the robot policy *and* the target MoCap data. A self-consistent autoencoder (trained with reconstruction + latent regularization, built on FLD) learns sparse structured motion representations that capture general patterns rather than nuances and noise; the latent dynamics model then generates modified reference motions and regularizes the bi-level loop. Explicitly motivated by references being "noisy and physically infeasible."
- **Verdict: 2-3 weeks. This is the most principled published version of "fix the reference with the policy in the loop."** More sample-efficient than BFMTrack's approach because the correction is amortized in a learned model.

**Others:** **CLOT**'s observation pre-shift (§1a) is a training-time version of the same intuition. **OmniTrack**'s Stage I (§1a) is an offline version. **HoRD**'s online distillation (§2) is the RMA-lineage version. **Dynamics as Prompts** https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.20357 for gradient-free online sysID. **FADA** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28476 (few-shot domain adaptation via dynamics alignment for humanoid control).

## 5. Perception-noise modeling for sim-to-real

Thin literature — nobody has published a principled 6D-pose-estimator error model for policy training. What exists:

- **ComPose: When to Trust Hands for Object Pose Tracking** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23523 (May 2026). Learns an adaptive fusion weight `α` between object-geometry evidence (masked point cloud, SAM3 + 3D foundation model) and hand-motion evidence, shifting toward hand motion when geometry is ambiguous under grasp occlusion. Predicts a **residual** correction `t̂_i = c_i + δ_i` off the masked point-cloud centroid rather than absolute translation. **Read this to understand the shape of the error your tier-3 references will have** — it is occlusion-driven, temporally bursty, and hand-correlated, not Gaussian.
- **6D Object Pose Tracking in Internet Videos for Robotic Manipulation** https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.10307 (ICLR 2025).
- **Benchmarking the Effects of Object Pose Estimation and Reconstruction on Robotic Grasping Success** https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17101 (Feb 2026) — empirical error→task-success curves, the closest thing to a calibrated noise budget.
- **Robust 6DoF Pose Estimation Against Depth Noise** https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.13570; **BOP Challenge 2024** https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.02812 (per-method error distributions on real data you can fit); **Conformal Keypoint / pose estimation with statistical guarantees** https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.12246; **AUGCAL** https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.06106 (sim2real uncertainty calibration).
- **CMP's OOD-Sensor recipe** (§2) and **CLONE's velocity-scaled SDE** (§1a) are the two best *usable* perception-noise models in the robot-learning literature right now. Neither was fit to a real estimator; both are structurally right.
- On the reconstruction side, know what your tier-3 pipeline will actually produce: **HaWoR** https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.02973, **Dyn-HaMR** https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.12861, **WHOLE** https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.22209, **EgoGrasp** https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.01050, **AGILE** https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04672, **HOT3D** https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.19167 (GT for calibrating your synthetic corruption).

## Synthesis: what I'd actually do in 6 weeks

Your stack: Isaac Lab + rsl_rl/skrl PPO. Best starting codebase is **BeyondMimic** (https://github.com/HybridRobotics/whole_body_tracking — Isaac Lab 2.1.0, rsl_rl, G1) or **HOVER** (https://github.com/NVlabs/HOVER — Isaac Lab + rsl_rl, masking modes). For retargeting, **GMR** (https://github.com/YanjieZe/GMR, MIT) explicitly supports **Unitree G1 + Dex3-1 hands, 7 DoF per hand, 43 DoF total**, and ingests monocular video via GVHMR — that is your tier-3 pipeline, already built.

**Week 1 — the free wins (all independently ablatable):**
1. RGMT command-noise table as an env wrapper, with a `noise_level` scalar so you can sweep it (§1a). Train on a *distribution* over noise levels, not a fixed one.
2. CLOT observation pre-shift: shift the observed reference window by `δ`, reward against unshifted (§1a). 20 lines.
3. Make all noise **temporally correlated / episode-level**, not per-step iid. Four independent papers report per-step resampling is worse.
4. Asymmetric critic: critic sees the clean reference, actor sees the corrupted one. Free variance reduction, and every 2026 paper does it.

**Week 2 — object-centric reward restructure.** Your scoring is object tracking accuracy, so stop making the policy imitate noisy hand trajectories. Adopt DexTrack's weighting (`w_obj,pos = 1.0` vs `w_hand,* = 0.1`) and DexMan's object-frame contact-prior reward (§1c). This is probably your largest single score gain on tiers 2-3 and it's independent of everything else.

**Week 2-3 — reference augmentation with preserved contact.** The `ΔT(t)` warp from 2607.11874 (§1c, 2-3 days) first, then InterReal's IK-repaired object offsets (§1c) if you have time. Both generate unlimited references that stay physically coherent, unlike raw Gaussian noise on joint targets.

**Week 3-4 — noise conditioning.** Compute DexTrack's `(t_s, t_c, t_p)` triple in a sliding window and append to the observation (§2, ~2 days). If that helps, upgrade to a StableHand-style Quality Network trained on your tier-1↔tier-2 pairs to regress per-frame reference error (§2, ~1 week). You have the paired supervision for free — most papers don't.

**Week 4-5 — pick one architectural upgrade:**
- **RGMT's dynamics-conditioned cross-attention command encoder** (§1a) if you want the best tracking-accuracy-per-hour. Their ablation says this is where the robustness lives.
- **GigaBrain's Mahalanobis command filter** (§1a) if you want hard protection against tier-3 catastrophic frames. Needs a GMM head over the command latent; `R_safe = 3` with radial retraction.
- **SA-PPO smoothness regularizer** (§3) if you want the paper to have a theory section. Cheapest of the three.

**Week 5-6 — test-time.** Pink-noise reference-sequence optimization in the spirit of BFMTrack, run directly on your reference rather than a BFM latent (§4, 24 iters × 128 envs, minutes per trajectory). Guard any online adaptation with BRIC's EMA-consistency loss (§4) so you don't trade tier-1 score for tier-3.

**Things to skip:** full CMP, SkillMimic-V2's STG, anything requiring a pretrained behavioral foundation model, ATLA-style learned adversaries (2× cost for marginal gain over SA-PPO at your scale), and OmniTrack's two-stage pipeline unless your tier-3 references are so broken that no single-stage policy converges — in which case it becomes the *first* thing to do, not the last.

**One framing note for the writeup:** the gap you're filling is real and citable. Every major humanoid tracking paper (PHC, H2O, OmniH2O, HumanPlus, ExBody2, GMT) claims robustness to noisy video-derived references while training exclusively on filtered clean data, and the three 2026 papers that do train with corrupted references (RGMT, CLOT, OmniTrack) have no released code and none of them handle hand-object interaction. A graded-noise benchmark on object tracking accuracy with a G1+Dex3 is unoccupied territory.
