# Report facts: Tier 3 reconstruction and the second final (2026-11-04 EST, for the extended abstract)

For rxzhou's `docs/report/main.tex` (branch `track2/p1-training`, draft 2 of 11-03). Each fact names its source; sources marked *local* are yqchen's notes outside the repo, so the numbers are copied here.

## 1. Paste-ready text (Tier 3 reconstruction paragraph, about 130 words)

> The CARI4D pipeline segments the video with Grounding DINO and SAM 2, estimates depth with MoGe, registers the object with FoundationPose and fits the SOMA body. FoundationPose keeps a pool of candidate poses per frame; we pick one path through the whole clip with a Viterbi search whose unary term is the log-softmax of the candidate's FoundationPose score plus five times its silhouette IoU, and whose pairwise term penalises rotation (per 10°) and translation (per 5 cm) between frames, symmetry-aware; an anchor bonus of 0.5 for candidates within 24° of FoundationPose's own pick keeps the exporter's choice unless the timeline cost prefers a different orientation by more than that bonus. Support surfaces are our own, built from the object's own static segments with MoGe confirming a surface lies beneath, never the released support files; objects that lean on furniture get a collision box where MoGe sees the surface they lean on.
>
> (For the second final add: "For two episodes the rotation penalty is doubled (per 5°) by an automatic per-episode rule; see §3.")

## 2. Facts and sources

**Inputs (Tier 3 rule compliance).** The chain reads only the exo video, the official object mesh and the `plane` field of the released ground file. It reads no Tier 1 or Tier 2 trajectory and not the released `support_surface/` (it sits under each clean resting pose, so it leaks rest positions). `tier_guard.forbid_lower_tiers()` blocks lower-tier paths by real path. Source: `agent-doc/overview-tier3-pipeline-2026-10-20.md` §2.
- Two grey areas the overview lists and never asked the organizers about: the ground file sits only in the Tier 1/2 directories (we read its `plane` field as a static calibration), and using the official object mesh is our reading of the rules. Whether the report mentions them is the team's call.
- The reconstruction itself does not use the Tier 1 frame-0 pose; the frame-0 alignment in the draft is done at packing. The overview dates the FAQ entry that allows it to **2026-10-19** (FAQ PDF commit `d9d6bf04`); the draft says "21 October". Please check which date the draft should cite.

**Timeline selector** (`stages/recovery/tier3/t3_fp_select.py`). Unary −(log-softmax of the FoundationPose score + 5 × silhouette IoU); pairwise rotation/(10° × gap) + translation/(5 cm × gap), rotation symmetry-aware; anchor: unary − `BETA` for states within `ANCHOR_DEG` 24° of FoundationPose's own pose; `BETA` 0.5 chosen by a pre-registered A/B over {0, 0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.5} on ep03/04/13 (judge: full-silhouette IoU; 0.2, 0.3, 0.5 tied, ties broken by fewest jumps). Used on the 13 episodes whose pick is FoundationPose's (ep19 excluded). Source: overview §9.2–9.3.

**Supports.** `t3_support.py`: static segments (within 5 cm of the median over 15 frames) off the floor become candidates; MoGe only decides whether a surface lies under the object and which placements share one; the height is the object's own (median of its lowest points), not MoGe's. Lean boxes (`gate/t3_lean_support.py`, `bc1d566`): an unheld segment that topples quasi-statically by more than 10° gets a box at the MoGe surface height, placed against the object; written only where the object never enters it elsewhere (ep01: two boxes on a bar-stool seat; ep28's candidates were rejected). Pre-training gate with our supports: 30/30. Source: overview §6.1; `agent-doc/delivery-tier3-gb6_best_dr2s_dyn7-2026-11-04.md`.

## 3. Second final (gb6): what differs and why

- **Reference change**: only ep11 (book flip) and ep17 (traffic cone). An automatic per-episode rule (`t3_rot_rule.py`, Tier 3 inputs only) runs the selector at `ROT_DEG` 10 (delivered) and 5, compares the silhouette IoU against the SAM 2 object mask (person pixels removed) on the frames where the two picks differ, and takes 5 only if it is higher by more than 0.02. Picked: ep11 (0.768 → 0.801 on 48 changed frames), ep17 (0.816 → 0.845, 28 frames). Source: `agent-doc/handoff-tier3-gb6-rot5-2026-11-04.md`, DELIVERY.
- **Policy change**: the first final's ep11/ep17 rows were trained on the **hm** reference (ep17 `t3-ep17-w2048-cap4m-r3a-hm-mf4090`; ep11 the 4,096,000-step placeholder). The second final retrains both with the same lines on gb6. So the two finals differ on these episodes by hm → gb6, not only by `ROT_DEG`.
- **Where 5 came from (external data, see §4)**: on a synthetic clip with 3D ground truth (ep03, picking up a mug), splitting the Viterbi cost showed the wrong chain was jerkier; doubling the rotation penalty (`ROT_DEG` 10 → 5) cut wrong hand-held frames (error ≥ 20°) from 83/152 to 0/152 on one reconstruction run, and left another run of the same clip (other machine, different FoundationPose branch) at 152/152. 5 was the only value tried before the competition data was run; a 3–8 sweep was run afterwards for reference and did not change the submission. Source: *local* `~/v2d/synth-hoi/report-tier3-tuning-2026-11-04.md` §4.2 (83/152 → 0/152) and *local* `~/v2d/synth-hoi/plan-tier3-rot5-submission-2026-11-04.md` §6.1 (152/152).
- **Evidence and limits** (state them; all in DELIVERY): applied to all 13 episodes, `ROT_DEG` 5 gave no net gain (answer frames 12/13 both; changed frames 215 better / 256 worse by silhouette, slightly negative by the judge), which is why it is gated per episode. The rule and its 0.02 margin were set after seeing these 13 episodes. A sweep over 3–8 selects ep17 at every value; the rule selects ep11 only at 5, though on the 48 frames it changes at 5 the gain is the same at 3 and 4 (one-sided plateau). On 8 frames where a human could tell the better orientation, the IoU judge agreed on 7 and was within the noise line on 1; on ep13 it disagrees with a human-labelled answer frame. ep17's delivered-layer dynamics violations rose 26 → 46 (not attributed). Both episodes lose the object in training on earlier references (ep11 ADD-AUC 0.069 on hm, 0.065 on gb5; ep17 MPPE 92 cm on hm; copied from your training records on `track2/p1-training`, please check against your logs), so no board gain is predicted.

## 4. External data (Kaggle rules §7)

The synthetic clips (three human–object scenes generated with full 3D truth in our own simulation, then restyled to a photographic look with Seedance, a commercial video model) were used only in development: to choose `ROT_DEG` 5 and to check candidate changes. They are not read by the submission pipeline and not needed in the container. They are not public; whether they meet §7.a/§7.b ("publicly available … at no cost" or "reasonably accessible … minimal cost") is a call for the team, so the report should state their origin and use plainly.
