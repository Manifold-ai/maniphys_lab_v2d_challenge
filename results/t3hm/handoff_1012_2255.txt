feat(tier3): depth-first object rest correction; support rules; replacement criteria

- t3_depth_post.py: rest frames meet their surface by a depth ratio along the object's own ray (capped at +-5 %, the rest
  vertical, one correction per rest segment); frames between rests interpolate the depth ratio only, so they keep their
  image position; clamps smoothed in time off rests. Humans follow the object's vertical move on held frames, never down
  past the feet (--move-human=3d follows all of it). Silhouette IoU on 29 episodes: vertical shift 0.737, this 0.807,
  uncorrected 0.810.
- t3_support.py: a support seen only through placements held throughout is dropped (held_only, ep28's fake 0.654 m
  seat); unheld static frames with nothing under them are on the floor (on_floor floor_runs, frame by frame: ep24's can
  is lifted for 566 of them). inside() drops repeated vertices: patches rounded to mm in the json had zero-length edges
  in 11 episodes, so every downstream support test (clamps, penetration counts) saw nothing inside.
- t3_height_post.py anchors on_floor floor runs.
- diagnosis: t3_depth_diag.py (depth error at rests, 1-3 %), t3_hand_depth_diag.py (the hand pins depth only within
  ~29 cm), t3_rest_split.py (rest wander is position, not orientation), t3_align_check.py (world poses back on the masks).
- gate/: chain_check.sh (all checks of a version), t3_post_verify.py (independent check from the parquet),
