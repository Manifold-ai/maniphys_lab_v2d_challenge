### Evaluation：1707 字符，sha256 681962b4fe0170be4803155485b03888d418ba7f67ae28cf6d86f1f3870dc8ec
- Submissions are evaluated on **MPPE**, the mean per-point position error, in centimetres.
- Six points are rigidly attached to each object at a radius of 5 cm along its body frame's principal axes. Each is carried into the world by that object's pose and compared against the same point on the reference:
- $$ \mathrm{MPPE}\_t = \frac{1}{6}\sum\_{k=1}^{6} \lVert (p\_t + R\_t v\_k) - (\hat{p}\_t + \hat{R}\_t v\_k) \rVert\_2 $$
- where the six \(v\_k\) are \(\pm 5\,\mathrm{cm}\) along each principal axis. Averaged over points, objects and frames within an episode, then over episodes.
- **Lower is better.** A rollout that reproduces the reference exactly scores 0.
- Because the points sit off the object's origin, orientation error shows up as position error: a rotation about the object's centre leaves the origin untouched but moves every keypoint. The 5 cm radius sets how strongly it is weighed. One object is enough to score it, so no episode is skipped.
